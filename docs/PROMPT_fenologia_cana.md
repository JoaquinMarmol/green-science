# Prompts — Ciclo fenológico de la CAÑA DE AZÚCAR (sin productos)

Dos prompts **separados**, cada uno genera su propia imagen: uno para **caña planta (caña hoja)** —de semilla, primer corte, ~12–18 meses— y otro para **caña soca** —rebrote tras el corte, ~12 meses—. Sin nombrar productos; solo las fases fenológicas (germinación, macollaje, gran crecimiento, maduración) con sus días después de plantación/corte (DAP). Referencia: *Saccharum officinarum*, fases fisiológicas estándar de la caña.

---

## 1) CAÑA PLANTA (CAÑA HOJA)

**Prompt (inglés, pegar tal cual):**

```
Educational botanical infographic, clean solid white background, flat vector
illustration style, horizontal composition, high resolution, no logo, no
watermark, NO product names, NO brands.

Top-left title in two parts: "CICLO FENOLÓGICO DE LA" in bold dark navy blue,
and below it "CAÑA PLANTA (CAÑA HOJA)" in a large thin light font.

Show ONE horizontal row with FIVE stages of a sugarcane plant (Saccharum
officinarum) grown from a HORIZONTAL seed-cane cutting (sett) with buds, left to
right, drawn to scale (small sprout at left, tall segmented cane at right).
Botanically accurate: jointed/segmented stalks with visible NODES and INTERNODES,
long arching green leaves from the nodes, fibrous roots below a horizontal soil
line at every stage. Stage name in bold black, day range in grey (Spanish),
each connected to the plant by a thin dashed green vertical line.

- Stage 1 — "GERMINACIÓN Y BROTACIÓN": a horizontal seed-cane sett underground
  with buds sprouting shoots upward and first roots (sprouts appear ~15–30 days).
  "0 – 60 días".
- Stage 2 — "MACOLLAJE (AHIJAMIENTO)": a clump of 5–10 tillers rising from the
  base of the mother shoot. "60 – 150 días".
- Stage 3 — "GRAN CRECIMIENTO (ELONGACIÓN)": tall, rapidly elongating stalks with
  many nodes and internodes and dense long leaves (the longest phase).
  "150 – 270 días".
- Stage 4 — "MADURACIÓN (ACUMULA SACAROSA)": thick tall canes, lower leaves
  drying, sugar accumulation. "270 – 420 días".
- Stage 5 — "COSECHA": full mature cane ready to cut. "12 – 18 meses".

Add a thin horizontal timeline arrow along the bottom with tick marks, labeled
"DÍAS DESPUÉS DE LA PLANTACIÓN (DAP)". Consistent green palette, realistic plant
proportions, clean modern layout, high resolution.
```

## 2) CAÑA SOCA (REBROTE)

**Prompt (inglés, pegar tal cual):**

```
Educational botanical infographic, clean solid white background, flat vector
illustration style, horizontal composition, high resolution, no logo, no
watermark, NO product names, NO brands.

Top-left title in two parts: "CICLO FENOLÓGICO DE LA" in bold dark navy blue,
and below it "CAÑA SOCA (REBROTE)" in a large thin light font.

Show ONE horizontal row with FIVE stages of a RATOON sugarcane crop (Saccharum
officinarum) that regrows from the CUT STUBBLE/STUMP left in the soil after
harvest, left to right, drawn to scale (small regrowth sprouts at left, tall
segmented cane at right). Botanically accurate: jointed/segmented stalks with
visible NODES and INTERNODES, long arching green leaves, fibrous roots below a
horizontal soil line; the first stage clearly shows a CUT STUMP/STUBBLE at ground
level with new shoots. Stage name in bold black, day range in grey (Spanish),
each connected to the plant by a thin dashed green vertical line.

- Stage 1 — "REBROTE DE LA SOCA": new shoots sprouting from the cut stubble/stool
  in the ground (faster start than plant cane). "0 – 45 días".
- Stage 2 — "MACOLLAJE": a clump of tillers from the ratoon stool. "45 – 120 días".
- Stage 3 — "GRAN CRECIMIENTO (ELONGACIÓN)": tall stalks elongating with many
  nodes and internodes. "120 – 240 días".
- Stage 4 — "MADURACIÓN (ACUMULA SACAROSA)": mature canes, sucrose peak, lower
  leaves drying. "240 – 330 días".
- Stage 5 — "COSECHA": mature cane ready to cut. "≈ 12 meses".

Add a thin horizontal timeline arrow along the bottom with tick marks, labeled
"DÍAS DESPUÉS DEL CORTE". Consistent green palette, realistic plant proportions,
clean modern layout, high resolution.
```

---

**Negative prompt (para ambos):**
```
no product names, no brands, no logos, no watermark, no text errors, no distorted
plants, realistic, not oversaturated, smooth stalks without nodes (cane stalks
must show nodes and internodes)
```

> Los días son orientativos. La caña planta (caña hoja) cierra ciclo en ~12–18
> meses; la soca en ~12 meses, con leve caída de rinde en cada rebrote. Ajustá los
> rangos según variedad, zona (Norte de Santa Cruz / Bermejo) y manejo.
