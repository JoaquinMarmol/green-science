# Prompt — Ciclo fenológico de la CHÍA (escala BBCH, sin productos)

Infografía del ciclo fenológico de la chía (*Salvia hispanica* L.) por días, **sin nombrar productos**. Basado en la **escala BBCH para chía** (Pérez Brandán et al., 2019): 8 estadios principales. Ciclo total ~**150–170 días**. La chía es de **día corto** (fotoperíodo): la fecha de floración se adelanta o atrasa según la latitud y la fecha de siembra; los días son orientativos.

**Prompt (inglés, pegar tal cual):**

```
Educational botanical infographic, clean solid white background, flat vector
illustration style, horizontal composition, high resolution, no logo, no
watermark, NO product names, NO brands.

Top-left title in two parts: "CICLO FENOLÓGICO DE LA" in bold dark navy blue,
and below it "CHÍA (Salvia hispanica)" in a large thin light font.

Show ONE horizontal row with EIGHT phenological stages of a chia plant, left to
right, drawn to scale (tiny seed at left, tall flowering then dry seed-bearing
plant at right). Botanically accurate: erect SQUARE stems, OPPOSITE serrated
green leaves in decussate pairs, and terminal/axillary flower spikes
(verticillasters) bearing small blue-violet two-lipped tubular flowers; show the
roots below a horizontal soil line at every stage.

Each stage has a callout connected by a thin dashed green line, with the BBCH
code and stage name in bold black and the approximate days in grey (Spanish):

- Stage 1 — "BBCH 0 · GERMINACIÓN": seed with radicle and emerging cotyledons
  below/at the soil line. "0 – 10 días".
- Stage 2 — "BBCH 1 · DESARROLLO DE HOJAS": seedling with 1–3 pairs of true
  leaves. "10 – 25 días".
- Stage 3 — "BBCH 2–3 · RAMIFICACIÓN": branching plant, several nodes, no flowers
  yet. "25 – 45 días".
- Stage 4 — "BBCH 5 · APARICIÓN DE INFLORESCENCIA": terminal verticillasters
  (flower spikes) forming at the tips. "45 – 60 días".
- Stage 5 — "BBCH 6 · FLORACIÓN": spikes covered with small blue-violet flowers
  (full flowering = BBCH 65). "60 – 90 días".
- Stage 6 — "BBCH 7 · FORMACIÓN DEL FRUTO": flowers fade, seeds (nutlets) forming
  inside the calyx. "85 – 115 días".
- Stage 7 — "BBCH 8 · MADURACIÓN": spikes turning brown, seeds filling and
  hardening. "110 – 150 días".
- Stage 8 — "BBCH 9 · MADUREZ / COSECHA": dry brown plant, spikes full of ripe
  seeds, ready to harvest. "150 – 170 días".

Add a thin horizontal timeline arrow along the bottom with tick marks, labeled
"DÍAS DESPUÉS DE LA SIEMBRA (DDS)". Consistent green palette, realistic plant
proportions, clean modern layout, high resolution.
```

**Negative prompt (opcional):**
```
no product names, no brands, no logos, no watermark, no text errors, no distorted
plants, realistic, not oversaturated, round stems (chia stems are square)
```

> Referencia: escala BBCH para chía — Pérez Brandán et al. (2019), *Scientia
> Horticulturae* 255:292–297. Temperatura base ~6 °C. Ajustá los días según
> variedad, zona (p. ej. Chiquitanía / Pailón, Santa Cruz) y fecha de siembra
> (la chía es sensible al fotoperíodo).
