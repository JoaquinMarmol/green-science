# Prompts IA — Foto del centro de cada etiqueta (Línea BioAgro)

Cada etiqueta tiene un recuadro central "FOTO DEL CULTIVO (a generar / colocar)". Generá una foto vertical (retrato, ~3:4) por producto con estos prompts y reemplazá el placeholder. Estilo común: fotografía agrícola profesional, luz natural, alta resolución, sin texto ni logos.

**Negative prompt (todos):** `no text, no words, no logos, no watermark, realistic, not oversaturated`

| Producto | Prompt (inglés) |
|---|---|
| **BioCore** (biofertilizante base) | `Close-up of dark rich living soil with young green seedlings emerging, crumbly organic matter and roots, morning light, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioNDrive** (nitrógeno) | `Vigorous green cereal crop (maize/wheat) in active vegetative growth, lush leaves, sunny field, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioFill** (fósforo/potasio) | `Healthy potato tubers and ripe fruits (tomato/pepper) full and uniform, freshly harvested on soil, warm light, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioBoost** (bioestimulante) | `Young vigorous plant with strong developed root system and early flowering, held in a hand, blurred green field, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioResista** (antiheladas) | `Green healthy crop leaves with frost/dew at sunrise, resilient plants in cold morning, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioSync** (coadyuvante) | `Agricultural sprayer applying over a green crop field at golden hour, fine mist, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioGuard** (biofungicida) | `Close-up of a perfectly healthy clean crop canopy, glossy disease-free green leaves, no spots, morning light, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioNodo** (semilla leguminosas) | `Close-up of a soybean/legume root system pulled from the soil showing white nitrogen-fixing nodules, natural light, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioSprout** (semilla pasturas/hortalizas) | `Rows of young seedlings emerging perfectly uniform in dark soil at sunrise, strong green shoots, dew, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioStrike** (bioinsecticida) | `Macro of a healthy undamaged green leaf with a beneficial ladybug, blurred sunlit crop behind, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioMollux** (molusquicida) | `Close-up of pristine intact vegetable leaves free of slug/snail damage with a snail nearby on the soil, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |
| **BioVita** (pecuaria) | `Healthy cattle grazing on a lush green pasture at golden hour with clean water, photorealistic, vertical 3:4` |

> Nota: la **composición** de cada producto la propuse espejando su equivalente de Green Science (marcada como orientativa). Revisala y ajustá los microorganismos/porcentajes reales de cada fórmula BioAgro antes de imprimir.
