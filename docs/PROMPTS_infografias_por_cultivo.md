# Prompts de infografía — uno individual por cultivo

Cada bloque es un prompt completo y autónomo (copiar y pegar tal cual en el generador de imágenes). Estilo igual a las de maíz/soya/sorgo/girasol.

## QUINUA

```
Educational agronomy infographic, clean solid white background, flat vector
illustration style, horizontal 4:3 composition, high resolution, no logo, no
watermark. Top-left title in two parts: "MANEJO INTEGRAL EN" in bold dark navy
blue, and below it "QUINUA" in a large thin light font.

Show one horizontal row with SIX growth stages of a quinoa plant (Chenopodium
quinoa), left to right, drawn to scale (small at left, tall at right), each with
visible roots below a horizontal soil line:
1) a small round quinoa seed germinating,
2) a young seedling with two cotyledons and the first goosefoot-shaped leaves,
3) a young branched plant with broad lobed green leaves,
4) a taller plant starting to form a terminal panicle,
5) a plant in flowering with a dense terminal panicle,
6) a mature plant with a large colorful panicle (magenta, orange and yellow grains).

Above each stage a callout connected to the plant by a thin dashed green vertical
line. Product names in bold dark green, doses in grey, stage name in bold black
(Spanish text):
- Stage 1: "SEED FORTE 3.0 500 ml/100 kg" — stage "SIEMBRA (TRAT. DE SEMILLA)"
- Stage 2: "FULL POWER 50 1 L/ha", "BIOMAX 43 1 L/ha" — stage "2 A 4 HOJAS"
- Stage 3: "FULL GREEN 100 1 L/ha", "BIOGUARD 1 L/ha", "BIOMAX 43 1 L/ha" — stage "RAMIFICACIÓN"
- Stage 4: "BIOGUARD 1 L/ha", "BIOMAX 43 1 L/ha" — stage "PANOJAMIENTO"
- Stage 5: "BIOGUARD 1 L/ha", "BIOMAX 43 1 L/ha" — stage "FLORACIÓN – GRANO LECHOSO"
- Stage 6: "FULL GREEN 100 0,5–1 L/ha" — stage "MADUREZ – POSTCOSECHA"

Consistent green palette, realistic plant proportions.
```
